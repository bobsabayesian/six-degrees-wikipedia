About This Project

This is a tool for turning the path between any two Wikipedia articles into plotter art.

I love Wikipedia because it captures a living reflection of who we are. The links we embed in an article map out not only the objective reality of a subject, but the architecture of our collective thoughts.

Every link in an article is a choice someone made. You have that same choice here. Through “via” nodes and the ignore list, you can tell or explore your own story about the connection between two ideas, within the limits of Wikipedia’s existing structure.

I created this space simply because I love plotter art and want everyone to be able to enjoy it. Please use it as much as you want for your own personal use, and support Wikipedia in any way you can. If you see a design you like, I will happily print and ship it to you.

More importantly, this project is driven by a deeper purpose. All profits from these prints, after materials and shipping, go directly toward a scholarship fund I am establishing in memory of two soldiers I served with and lost in Iraq: Coby Glen Schwab and Kelly Branden Grothe. Both of these men were tremendous leaders and soldiers, and I miss them every day.

The Scholarship Vision

I was able to attend high school and college because of needs-based scholarships, so I know firsthand the trajectory-changing impact that kind of support can have.

My goal is to work with a school to fund a starting scholarship for students who demonstrate both academic excellence and leadership—the exact traits Coby and Kelly brought to everything they did. I’m still sorting out the exact logistics of the fund, but I am committed to transparency. I will update this page regularly with our monthly fundraising totals so you can see exactly what we are building together.

To be upfront: this isn’t a registered charity yet, so contributions aren’t tax-deductible.
